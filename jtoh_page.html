H

Item Type

Letter

Obtained From

Bought with 100 in the Shop

Item Use

Creates a point-light (and a secret other power.)

Additional Information

Date Added

13 November 2021

H is an item in EToH that can be purchased in the Shop for 100 . Just like other letters, equipping the item creates a pointlight around your character allowing you to see clearer in dark areas.

Trivia

  • This item is the only letter that is currently obtainable.
  • The icon has a youtuber named Scouthex on it who is known for his series called "EToH Without Taking Damage".
