Tower of Annoyingly Simple Trials (ToAST(C)) is an Easy difficulty, ascension-based Tower located in Summit of Memories, formerly located in 100M Replay and Ring 1. It was made by Karinriine. It served as a tutorial to help new players understand the basics, mechanics, and advanced techniques found in EToH. It was replaced by Tower of A Simple Time for being considered a low-quality tutorial.

Skips

If you look closely at certain points in the tower, you can find extremely transparent blocks that allow you to skip certain parts of the tower.

  • You can skip the short 2 stud path on Floor 2 by walking on an invisible path.
  • You can skip the tightrope section on Floor 6 by going to a semi-visible path and a ladder.
  • You can skip the first outside section on Floor 7 by climbing an invisible truss.
  • Similar to the one above, you can skip the darkness part on Floor 7 by walking on an invisible path. However, due to the darkness, it is difficult to see.
  • You can skip the loose platforms tutorial on Floor 8 by climbing an invisible truss.
  • There is a ledge under the timed button section on Floor 9. If you go down it, there will be a portal that teleports you to Floor 10.
  • There is a 3 stud wide invisible path that you can take to skip the last outside section on Floor 9.

History

  • Sometime in 2020, an arrow path was added with the goal of guiding new players to the tower's portal.[b] A "RECOMMENDED FOR BEGINNERS" hint was also added on the floor, suggesting users beat this as their first tower of any type. Once users beat ToAST(C), the arrows and the sign would disappear.
  • On 21 February 2025, arrows were added inside ToAST(C) in order to help guide beginners, along with other Quality of Life changes.
  • On 14 June 2025, ToAST(C) was added to 100M Replay with its previous acronym, ToAST(R), in advance to prepare for its eventual removal from Ring 1.
  • On August 17 2025, the ToAST from Ring 1 is replaced with Tower of A Simple Time.
  • On November 11 2025, ToAST(R) was removed from 100M Replay after a subrealm dedicated to pre-revamped towers was confirmed.
  • On January 12 2026, ToAST(C) was readded to Summit of Memories.

Revamp

  • This tower was planned to get a revamp; it would have taught players mechanics that the current version does not include.
    • When Karinriine added the tower's revamp portal to the Beginner Towers section, it was possible to teleport to the unfinished revamp by entering the portal. However, this was eventually fixed.
      • It was impossible to beat the revamp, as there was a neon-red block in place of a winpad.
    • When the Tower Races update was released, users could play the unfinished revamp during a race. However, this was eventually fixed.[citation needed]
    • An archive of the ToAST(C) revamp can be found here.

Trivia

  • As this tower is intended to be a tutorial for new players, it had a exclusive "Tutorial" hint above its portal sign.
  • This tower has been beaten over a million times.
    • The millionth winner of this was AAA_A1a.[1]
